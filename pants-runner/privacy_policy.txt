========================================================================
1. OVERVIEW
========================================================================

This Privacy Policy explains how Pants Runner ("the App", "we", "us",
or "our") accesses, collects, uses, stores, and protects information
when you use the Pants Runner mobile game distributed via Google Play
and other platforms.

Pants Runner is designed as a privacy-first, completely offline game
for players of all ages. We do not collect, transmit, rent, sell, or
share any personal or sensitive user data with third parties, analytics
providers, or advertising networks.

========================================================================
2. INFORMATION WE ACCESS AND STORE
========================================================================

A. Locally Stored Game Data (On-Device Only)
--------------------------------------------
When you play Pants Runner, the App stores the following gameplay
progress and preferences locally on your device using the operating
system's sandboxed application storage (Android SharedPreferences / iOS
NSUserDefaults):

  - Highest cleared level (Levels 1 through 5)
  - Selected and unlocked cosmetic pants styles (e.g., Sky Denim up to
    Starry Purple)
  - Total completed runs count
  - In-game preferences (such as whether Haptic Vibration is enabled)

This data stays exclusively on your device so your unlocked pants styles
and preferences persist between sessions. The App requests no network or
internet permissions, never transmits gameplay data off your device, and
we have no access to it.

B. Optional Adult-Gated Feedback (External Email Client)
--------------------------------------------------------
Pants Runner includes an optional "Send Feedback" option inside the
Settings menu. The App itself does not make background network requests
or send emails automatically.

To protect younger players, tapping "Send Feedback" displays an "Adult
Check" arithmetic challenge. Only after an adult solves this check does
the App open a standard "mailto:" link in your device's external email
application addressed to staryellowmonkey+feedback@gmail.com. To help
diagnose technical issues, the pre-populated email draft includes the
following non-personal diagnostic header in the message body, which you
can inspect, edit, or delete before deciding whether to send:

  - Application name and version number (e.g., Pants Runner v1.0.0)
  - Operating system name and version
  - Device manufacturer and model name
  - A non-identifying cryptographic verification token (generated from
    a timestamp and random nonce with zero user or device identifiers)

No information leaves your device unless an adult explicitly chooses to
send the email from their own email client. If you voluntarily send us a
support email, we receive your email address, the message you write, and
the diagnostic header in that email solely to respond to your inquiry,
troubleshoot bugs, and improve the App.

C. Information We Do NOT Collect
--------------------------------
Pants Runner does NOT collect, access, or track:
  - Name, physical address, phone number, or account credentials
  - Precise or coarse geographic location (GPS or network location)
  - Contacts, calendar, photos, media, microphone, or camera data
  - Advertising IDs, analytics telemetry, or behavioral profiles
  - Financial, payment, or health information

========================================================================
3. DEVICE PERMISSIONS AND PLATFORM CAPABILITIES
========================================================================

Pants Runner requests only the minimal system capabilities needed for
offline gameplay and optional adult-gated support emails:

  - Vibration / Haptic Feedback (android.permission.VIBRATE):
    Used solely on-device for optional tactile vibration feedback during
    jumps, rail grinds, and bumps. You can turn off Haptic Vibration at
    any time in the Settings dialog.

  - Email Intent Query (android.intent.action.SENDTO / VIEW for
    mailto:):
    Used solely to locate and open your device's external email client
    when an adult explicitly taps "Send Feedback" in Settings and passes
    the Adult Check prompt.

Pants Runner does NOT request internet access permissions or Advertising
ID permissions.

========================================================================
4. DATA SHARING AND THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURES
========================================================================

  - We do NOT share, sell, rent, or trade any user data with third
    parties.
  - The App contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs,
    crash reporters, or social trackers.
  - If an adult voluntarily sends a feedback email, that correspondence
    is processed through standard email infrastructure (Gmail / Google
    Workspace) and is accessed only by the developer for support and
    quality assurance. We may only disclose information if strictly
    required by applicable law or valid legal process.

========================================================================
5. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION POLICY
========================================================================

A. On-Device Gameplay Data
--------------------------
All unlocked levels, pants selections, run counters, and haptic settings
remain exclusively on your device until you choose to clear them. You
can delete this data at any time by:
  1. Opening Settings inside Pants Runner and tapping "Reset Progress"
     (with confirmation) to reset unlocked levels, pants styles, and run
     history to initial defaults;
  2. Clearing the App's storage/data in your device's system settings;
     or
  3. Uninstalling Pants Runner from your device, which permanently
     removes all locally stored application data.

B. Voluntary Support & Feedback Emails
--------------------------------------
If an adult has voluntarily sent a feedback or support email to us, we
retain the correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the inquiry
and maintain quality assurance records. You may request permanent
deletion of your email and associated support record at any time by
contacting:

  staryellowmonkey+feedback@gmail.com

Upon receiving a deletion request, we will permanently delete your email
and associated support records within 30 days.

========================================================================
6. DATA SECURITY
========================================================================

All game progression and settings created in Pants Runner are stored
locally within the operating system's sandboxed application storage on
your device. When an adult chooses to send optional feedback through
their external email client, transport security (such as TLS encryption
in transit) is provided by their email client and email service
provider.

========================================================================
7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA, GDPR-K & GOOGLE PLAY FAMILIES)
========================================================================

Pants Runner is designed to be safe and enjoyable for players of all
ages, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of
digital consent in your jurisdiction).

  - The App does not require user accounts, sign-in, or personal
    information, and collects zero personal data, analytics, or
    persistent device identifiers.
  - The App contains no third-party advertisements or external web
    browsing links during gameplay.
  - Optional external feedback ("mailto:") is protected behind a neutral
    "Adult Check" arithmetic parental gate so children do not leave the
    App without adult involvement.

If a parent or guardian believes a child has voluntarily sent personal
information via support email, please contact us at
staryellowmonkey+feedback@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.
