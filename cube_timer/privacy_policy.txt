========================================================================
1. OVERVIEW
========================================================================

This Privacy Policy explains how PlusTwo Timer ("the App", "we", "us",
or "our") accesses, collects, uses, stores, and protects information
when you use the PlusTwo Timer mobile application distributed via Google
Play and other platforms.

PlusTwo Timer is designed to operate as a privacy-first, offline-capable
speedcubing stopwatch and multi-column comparison tool. We do not
automatically collect, transmit, rent, sell, or share any personal or
sensitive user data with third parties, analytics providers, or
advertising networks.

========================================================================
2. INFORMATION WE ACCESS AND STORE
========================================================================

A. Locally Stored Application Data (On-Device Only)
---------------------------------------------------
When you use PlusTwo Timer, the App stores the following operational
data locally on your device using the operating system's sandboxed
application storage (Android SharedPreferences / iOS NSUserDefaults):

  - Custom column or round names (e.g., "Round 1", "Round 2")
  - Recorded and manually entered solve times
  - Active column and row configuration
  - Application settings (such as whether Haptic Feedback is enabled)

This data is stored exclusively on your device so your session persists
between app launches. It is never transmitted off your device by the
App, and we have no access to it.

B. Optional User-Initiated Feedback (External Email Client)
-----------------------------------------------------------
PlusTwo Timer includes an optional "Send Feedback" button inside the
Settings menu. The App itself does not transmit background network
requests or emails.

If you voluntarily tap "Send Feedback" -> "Email", the App uses a
standard "mailto:" link to open your device's external email application
addressed to staryellowmonkey+feedback@gmail.com. To help us diagnose
technical issues, the pre-populated email draft includes the following
non-personal diagnostic information in the message body, which you can
review, edit, or delete before sending:

  - Application name and version number (e.g., PlusTwo Timer v1.0.4)
  - Operating system name and version (e.g., Android release and SDK
    version)
  - Device manufacturer and model name
  - A non-identifying cryptographic verification token (generated from
    a timestamp and random nonce with zero user or device identifiers)

No information is sent unless you explicitly choose to send the email
from your own email client. If you send us an email, we will receive
your email address, the message you write, and the diagnostic header
included in the email body. We use this information solely to respond to
your inquiry, troubleshoot reported bugs, and improve the App.

C. Information We Do NOT Collect
--------------------------------
PlusTwo Timer does NOT collect, access, or track:
  - Name, physical address, phone number, or account credentials
  - Precise or coarse geographic location (GPS or network location)
  - Contacts, calendar, photos, media, microphone, or camera data
  - Advertising IDs, analytics telemetry, or behavioral tracking profiles
  - Financial, payment, or health information

========================================================================
3. DEVICE PERMISSIONS AND PLATFORM CAPABILITIES
========================================================================

PlusTwo Timer requests only the minimum system capabilities required for
its core features:

  - Vibration / Haptic Feedback (android.permission.VIBRATE):
    Used solely on-device to provide optional tactile vibration feedback
    when arming, starting, and stopping the stopwatch timer. You can
    disable haptic feedback at any time in the App's Settings menu.

  - Email Intent Query (android.intent.action.SENDTO / mailto:):
    Used solely to check for and open your external email application
    when you explicitly tap the "Send Feedback" button in Settings.

========================================================================
4. DATA SHARING AND THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURES
========================================================================

  - We do NOT share, sell, rent, or trade any user data with third
    parties.
  - The App contains no third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or
    social media trackers.
  - If you voluntarily send us a feedback email, your email is processed
    through standard email infrastructure (Gmail / Google Workspace) and
    is accessed only by the developer for support and quality assurance
    purposes. We may only disclose information if strictly required to
    do so by applicable law or valid legal process.

========================================================================
5. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION POLICY
========================================================================

A. On-Device Timer & Session Data
---------------------------------
All solve times, round names, and preferences remain on your device
until you choose to remove them. You can delete this data at any time
by:
  1. Tapping any solve cell or column to delete individual times or
     columns inside the App;
  2. Tapping the "Clear All" button in the top bar of the App;
  3. Clearing the App's storage/data in your device's system settings; or
  4. Uninstalling PlusTwo Timer from your device, which permanently
     removes all locally stored application data.

B. Voluntary Support & Feedback Emails
--------------------------------------
If you have voluntarily sent a feedback or support email to us, we
retain the correspondence only as long as necessary to resolve your
inquiry and maintain quality assurance records. You may request complete
deletion of your feedback email and email address at any time by
contacting us at:

  staryellowmonkey+feedback@gmail.com

Upon receiving a deletion request, we will permanently delete your email
and associated support records within 30 days.

========================================================================
6. DATA SECURITY
========================================================================

All session and timer data created in PlusTwo Timer is stored locally
within the operating system's private, sandboxed application directory
on your device. When you choose to send optional feedback via your
external email provider, transmission security (such as TLS encryption
in transit) is handled by your configured email client and email
service provider.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
========================================================================

PlusTwo Timer is a general-audience utility application and does not
knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age
of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).
If a parent or guardian believes that a child has sent personal
information to us via a support email, please contact us at
staryellowmonkey+feedback@gmail.com and we will promptly delete the
information.
