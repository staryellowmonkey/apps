1. OVERVIEW
========================================================================

This Privacy Policy explains how Panda Pocket ("the App", "we", "us",
or "our") accesses, collects, uses, stores, and protects information
when you use the Panda Pocket mobile application distributed via Google
Play and other platforms.

Panda Pocket is a privacy-focused virtual money ledger designed for
parents to track allowance, chores, virtual checking and savings
balances, and savings goals on their device. Panda Pocket does not
connect to any bank or financial institution, does not process real
currency, and does not sell, rent, or share any user data with
advertising networks or data brokers.

========================================================================
2. INFORMATION WE ACCESS AND STORE
========================================================================

A. On-Device Storage & Automatic OS Device Backup
-------------------------------------------------
The App stores operational ledger data locally inside the operating
system's private, sandboxed application directory using a normalized
local SQLite database (`sqflite`) and platform preferences
(`SharedPreferences` / `NSUserDefaults`):

  - Selected currency symbol and optional salted HMAC-SHA256 hash of
    your 4-digit Parent PIN lock (your plaintext PIN is never stored)
  - Configured Checking and Savings virtual interest rates, payout
    period settings, and weekly/monthly chore payout schedules
  - Child profile display names, selected mascot avatars, virtual
    Checking and Savings balances, savings goal titles/targets, and
    daily/weekly/monthly chore titles, rewards, and completion records
  - Recorded virtual transactions (deposits, expenses, transfers,
    chore batch payouts, automatic interest entries, transaction notes,
    and annulment reasons)
  - When optional Parent Cloud Sync is used, local per-vault snapshots
    (stripped of local PIN and session secrets) and remembered shared
    vault references are cached in `SharedPreferences` to allow seamless
    switching between your personal vault and accepted shared vaults
  - When optional Google Drive Sheet Backup is enabled by a parent, the
    spreadsheet ID and worksheet tab metadata for the backup sheet are
    cached in `SharedPreferences`

On Android and iOS devices where operating-system device backup
(Google Play / Google One Auto Backup or Apple iCloud Backup) is
enabled by the user, the operating system automatically backs up your
local SQLite ledger database (`panda_pocket_ledger.db`) and app
preferences (`FlutterSharedPreferences.xml`) to your personal OS backup
account for device recovery without requiring in-app sign-in.

B. Optional Multi-Parent Cloud Sync & Anonymous Kid Device Linking
------------------------------------------------------------------
If a parent voluntarily chooses to enable multi-parent Cloud Sync in the
"Family Sync & Child Device Setup" menu, the App uses authenticated
Google Sign-In (`google_sign_in` and `firebase_auth`), Firebase App
Check (`firebase_app_check`), and Google Cloud Firestore
(`cloud_firestore`) to synchronize your household vault across
authorized parent devices and optional read-only kid devices:

  - Parent Account Identifiers & Dual-Consent Protection: Your verified
    Google Account email address and any co-parent Google Account email
    addresses you explicitly authorize. Joining a shared family vault
    requires dual consent: (1) the primary vault owner must pre-authorize
    the co-parent's email address, and (2) the co-parent must enter the
    inviting parent's email address.
  - Anonymous Single-Use Kid Code Linking (Read-Only): Parents can
    optionally link a child's device in read-only mode using the
    parent's email address and a single-use 6-character Kid Code. The
    child's device connects via Firebase Anonymous Authentication
    (`signInAnonymously()`) without ever asking for or collecting a
    child's email address or Google Account.
  - Synchronized Household Vault: Your currency symbol, interest and
    savings account settings, child profile first names/nicknames,
    mascot avatars, virtual balances, savings goals, chore definitions
    and completion records, linked read-only device labels, and virtual
    transaction history (stored using zlib-compressed ledger payloads to
    minimize cloud storage footprint).
  - Security Exclusion: Your local Parent PIN hash and local view-mode
    state are stripped and never uploaded to Cloud Firestore.

All Cloud Firestore communication is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS
and restricted by Firebase Security Rules to authenticated, verified
Google Accounts listed in your household's authorized member list (and
read-only access for anonymous kid device UIDs explicitly linked via a
single-use Kid Code).

C. Optional Write-Only Google Drive Sheet Backup (`drive.file` Scope)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Parents who are signed in with Google can optionally enable the
"Google Drive Sheet Backup" toggle in the Sync menu (OFF by default).
When enabled by the parent:
  - The App requests the narrow OAuth 2.0 scope
    `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file`, which grants access
    solely to the single spreadsheet file (`Panda Pocket Backup`)
    created by Panda Pocket in the parent's Google Drive.
  - The App performs write-only updates via the Google Sheets API
    (`https://sheets.googleapis.com/v4/spreadsheets`) to maintain one
    worksheet tab per child containing their recorded Activity & Notes
    (`Date`, `Note`, `Account`, `Category`, `Amount`, `Status`). The App
    never reads or accesses any other files in your Google Drive.
  - Panda Pocket's use and transfer of information received from Google
    APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data
    Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

D. Optional User-Initiated Feedback (External Email Client)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Panda Pocket includes an optional "Send Feedback" option inside the
Household Settings menu. If you voluntarily tap "Send Feedback" ->
"Email", the App uses a standard "mailto:" link to open your device's
external email application addressed to
staryellowmonkey+feedback@gmail.com. To help us diagnose technical
issues, the pre-populated email draft includes non-personal diagnostic
metadata (App version, OS version, device model, and a non-identifying
verification token) which you can review, edit, or delete before
sending.

E. Information We Do NOT Collect
--------------------------------
Panda Pocket does NOT collect, access, or track:
  - Children's email addresses, phone numbers, or personal identifiers
  - Real bank accounts, credit cards, or real-world financial data
  - Precise or coarse geographic location (GPS or network location)
  - Contacts, calendar, photos, media, microphone, or camera data
  - Advertising IDs, analytics telemetry, or behavioral tracking profiles

5. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION POLICY
========================================================================

A. On-Device Ledger Data
------------------------
All household profiles, virtual balances, savings goals, and transaction
notes stored locally on your device remain until you remove them. You
can delete local data at any time by:
  1. Editing or removing individual child profiles or goals inside the
     App;
  2. Clearing the App's storage/data in your device's system settings; or
  3. Uninstalling Panda Pocket from your device, which removes all
     locally stored SQLite and preference data on the device (and you
     can manage or delete OS-level Google Play / Google One or iCloud
     app backups in your device's system backup settings).

B. Cloud Sync Data (In-App & Email Deletion)
--------------------------------------------
If you have enabled optional Cloud Sync, you can permanently delete your
household document (including its compressed cloud ledger payload) from
Firebase Cloud Firestore (or unlink your co-parent account) at any time:
  1. Directly inside the App: Open the "Family Sync" sheet (`cloud_sync`
     icon in the top bar) and tap "Delete Cloud Data". If you are the
     household owner, this immediately deletes the entire household
     record and its invite-code lookup entry from Cloud Firestore,
     purges all local cached vault snapshots and accepted-vault
     references, and signs out your Google Account. (If you also
     enabled the optional Google Drive Sheet Backup, the `Panda Pocket
     Backup` spreadsheet in your personal Google Drive remains under your
     control until you remove it in Google Drive.) If you joined as an
     invited co-parent, you may either tap "Leave" under "Active &
     Available Family Vaults" (to unlink from a shared vault while
     keeping your own personal vault) or tap "Delete Cloud Data" (which
     removes your email address from the shared household's authorized
     member list, clears local cached vault snapshots, and signs out your
     Google Account without deleting the primary owner's household vault).
  2. By Email Request: You may also request deletion of your cloud
     household data or removal of your account without using the App by
     emailing staryellowmonkey+feedback@gmail.com from your registered
     Google Account email address. We will permanently process the
     deletion request within 30 days.

C. Voluntary Support & Feedback Emails
--------------------------------------
If you have voluntarily sent a feedback or support email to us, you may
request complete deletion of your email correspondence at any time by
contacting staryellowmonkey+feedback@gmail.com.

========================================================================
6. DATA SECURITY
========================================================================

  - On-Device Storage: Stored in the operating system's sandboxed
    application directory (`sqflite` SQLite database). Any configured
    Parent PIN is hashed using salted HMAC-SHA256 (`PinSecurity`).
  - Cloud Sync Storage: Encrypted in transit via TLS/HTTPS and at rest
    by Google Cloud Firestore, governed by authenticated Firebase
    Security Rules.

========================================================================
7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
========================================================================

Panda Pocket is a parent-managed household ledger application. We do not
knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age
of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).
Child Mode is a read-only display lock (available locally on a parent's
device or on a child's device via an anonymous single-use Kid Code) and
does not solicit or transmit children's email addresses, Google
Accounts, or personal identifiers.
If a parent or guardian believes that a child has submitted personal
information to us, please contact us at
staryellowmonkey+feedback@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.
